Privacy Policy — Pop Girl Files Content Scheduler

Last updated: 22 September 2026

The Pop Girl Files Content Scheduler ("the app") is a personal publishing tool that runs locally on the operator's own computer. It is used solely by the operator of the Pop Girl Files channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What the app accesses

With your authorization, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, publish times and content disclosures on the connected channel, and to read the channel's name to confirm the connection. It requests only the scopes needed for this: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.

Data storage

OAuth tokens and app settings are stored in plain files on the operator's own computer (in the app's folder). Nothing is transmitted to any server other than the platform APIs themselves (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram). The app has no backend, no analytics, no third-party services, and collects no personal data from viewers or any other person.

Data sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Video files and metadata are sent only to the platform you choose, at your instruction.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions, and by clicking "Disconnect" in the app, which deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel operator via the Pop Girl Files YouTube channel's "About" page.
